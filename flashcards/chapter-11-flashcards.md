## Chapter 11: Full Exam Simulation and 80-Minute Time-Management Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | PCNSE time budget per question (average) | 80 minutes ÷ up to 75 questions ≈ 60-65 sec/question. Bank 15-20 sec on quick questions to build a buffer for scenario-heavy ones. |
| 2 | Minimum time to reserve for the final review pass | ~10 minutes. Use this window only to revisit flagged questions — not to start fresh analysis on new material. |
| 3 | Time threshold that should trigger 'flag and move on' | If you have no clear leading answer after ~90 seconds, flag it and move on — do not sink further time into one question. |
| 4 | Ideal number of flagged questions by the end of the exam | Aim for roughly 10-15 flags on a ~75-question exam. Flagging 20+ leaves too little time to meaningfully re-analyze each one during the review window. |
| 5 | Scenario mentions 'centralized management, logging, and reporting for many firewalls' | Points to Panorama. Exam tip: 'centralized' + 'multiple firewalls' = Panorama almost every time. |
| 6 | Scenario mentions 'both firewalls actively forward traffic with no session loss on failover' | Points to Active/Active HA with session/data-path synchronization — not simple Active/Passive. |
| 7 | Scenario mentions 'decrypt general traffic but exempt finance/health categories' | Points to SSL Decryption policy exclusion by URL Category — not a blanket decrypt-all or decrypt-nothing policy. |
| 8 | Two answer options both technically satisfy the requirement — how to choose? | Find the qualifier word in the stem: 'least overhead', 'most secure', 'lowest cost', 'minimum rules'. The qualifier decides the single best answer, not raw feasibility. |
| 9 | Active/Passive vs Active/Active HA — exam default assumption | Active/Passive is the simpler default unless the stem explicitly requires both firewalls processing traffic simultaneously (then it's Active/Active). |
| 10 | Correct order to read a PCNSE exam question | Read the full stem first and note every qualifier word, THEN read the options. Reading options first causes anchoring on a plausible-but-incomplete answer. |
| 11 | Common anchoring trap pattern in scenario questions | A distractor satisfies only the first clause of the stem (e.g., 'regardless of port') but ignores a later constraint (e.g., 'block unknown traffic' or 'minimum rules'). Always check every clause before selecting. |
| 12 | Best action with 15 min left and unanswered + flagged questions remaining | Finish all unanswered questions first (every question is equal weight, blank = zero credit), THEN spend remaining time on flagged review. |
