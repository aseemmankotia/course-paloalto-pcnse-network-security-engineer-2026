# Chapter 10 Lab: Structured Troubleshooting: Packet Flow, Logs, and Packet Captures

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-understand walkthrough: given a described connectivity failure symptom, select the correct diagnostic order (test security-policy-match, then check session table, then flow-basic capture) to isolate the root cause

## Key takeaway

Effective PCNSE-level troubleshooting means moving methodically from policy-match tests to session tables to packet captures, letting the specific log fields and capture stage point directly to the root cause.
