# Chapter 1 Lab: Single-Pass Architecture and Packet Flow Fundamentals

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-understand walkthrough: trace a single HTTPS session through the official PAN-OS packet flow diagram, marking each decision point (zone lookup, policy lookup, App-ID re-check, Content-ID)

## Key takeaway

PAN-OS packet flow is a defined sequence — zone lookup, session lookup, policy match, App-ID, then Content-ID — and understanding that order is the foundation for every other exam domain.
