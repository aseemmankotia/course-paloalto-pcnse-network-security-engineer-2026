# Chapter 9 Lab: GlobalProtect, Certificate Management, and Logging

**Estimated time:** 12 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-understand walkthrough: trace a GlobalProtect connection sequence from client to portal to gateway, identifying where certificate validation and HIP checks occur

## Key takeaway

GlobalProtect access, decryption, and management-plane trust all depend on correctly managed certificates, and portal/gateway roles plus log forwarding profiles must each be configured deliberately — nothing works by default.
